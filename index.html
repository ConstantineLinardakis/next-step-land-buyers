What kinds of properties do you buy?+

We consider vacant land, residential lots, acreage, and homes in a range of conditions nationwide. Share what you own, including any access, tax, or title issues you’re aware of, and we’ll take a look.

How do you determine a cash offer?+

We review location, size, access, condition, available property records, and comparable sales. Each offer is specific to the property and subject to review.

Am I obligated to accept an offer?+

No. Requesting an offer is free, and you can take time to consider your options. You decide whether selling to us is the right next step.

How can I learn more about a property for sale?+

Open any property card to explore its photos and details, then select “View on Zillow” for the full listing and current availability. You can also email us with the property address.

Do I need to prepare the property first?+

You can request an offer with the property in its current condition. There’s no need to clean up, make repairs, or arrange a visit just to start a conversation. Any necessary next steps can be discussed during review.
